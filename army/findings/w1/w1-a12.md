## Notes
- `effectcraft` is the product's own name and its internal crates are `effectcraft_*` throughout; the 30+ EFFECTCRAFT hits above are a sample of the pattern (ripgrep counts 181 occurrences across editor/crates alone). Removal is not a per-file fix: it needs a monorepo-wide crate/brand rename or a brand-constant pass. Reported lines are the per-file anchors a fix wave can start from.
- Recurring BADCODE pattern worth one systemic fix: `serde_json::to_string(...).unwrap_or_default()` paired with `from_json("") → None/default` in save paths (stabilize.rs:113, roto/mod.rs:110-118, camtrack/mod.rs:98-113,342-350, keyframe/value.rs:479, project/lib.rs:1544-1550). Every one of these converts a save/serialization failure into silent *data loss* (analysis/solve/strokes read back as absent). A shared `to_json_checked() -> Option<String>` helper plus refusing saves would fix all of them.
- VectorCraft references (path/boolean.rs, fit.rs, offset.rs) and the `app: photocraft|vectorcraft|filmcraft…` list in engine/src/commands/help.rs:50 indicate fork lineage with sibling products; decide whether those names are ours to keep in docs.
- Two test-only env vars carry the brand (`EFFECTCRAFT_FACE_LANDMARKER` in 3 crates, `EC_CAMTRACK_*` in camtrack) — fold into one naming scheme when the brand pass happens.
- Overall quality of these crates is high: hostile-input fuzz-style tests, issue-number-referenced regressions (#172/#274/#324/#367/#368/#408/#474/#482/#494), `#![deny(unwrap/expect/panic/todo)]` nearly everywhere. The genuine bug candidates are: project/lib.rs `comp_contains` unbounded recursion (cycle in a corrupt file), and convert.rs unchecked decoder-plane slicing. Everything else is hardening/brand work.
- roto/tests.rs (track/src/roto/tests.rs) is listed under neither S113 nor any other scope I could see in the manifest excerpt; I did not audit it — flagging so the fix wave assigns it.
