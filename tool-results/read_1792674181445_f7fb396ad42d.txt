     1→# Studio UI Design System
     2→
     3→Status: **accepted direction** · 2026-10-21 · Implementation not started
     4→
     5→The visual target is a **dark, cinematic, editorial SaaS** — the polish of a film title
     6→sequence, the clarity of a serious creative tool. It replaces the current generic
     7→studio dashboard rather than restyling it.
     8→
     9→> **Hard boundary:** this design system styles the **SaaS shell and the chat overlay**.
    10→> It does **not** style EffectCraft's editor. The editor UI is frozen upstream. We do
    11→> not inject CSS, change its theme, resize its panels, or “improve” its density. The
    12→> chat is designed to sit naturally over the editor, not to recolour it.
    13→
    14→---
    15→
    16→## 1. Design principles
    17→
    18→1. **Cinema, not dashboard.** The product is about motion, time and composition. The
    19→   shell should feel like a title sequence: deliberate typography, deep blacks, one
    20→   signal colour, and room to breathe.
    21→2. **The work is the hero.** Project thumbnails, frames and renders get the colour and
    22→   scale. Chrome stays quiet.
    23→3. **One accent, used like a laser.** A single warm signal colour for action and state.
    24→   No rainbow gradients, no neon-on-neon, no decorative glow.
    25→4. **Editorial contrast.** A serif display face against a precise sans UI creates the
    26→   film-poster feel without illustration or 3D blobs.
    27→5. **Dense where it matters.** The editor and timeline are dense; the SaaS shell is
    28→   spacious. Do not make the shell look like a spreadsheet.
    29→6. **Motion with intent.** 120–240 ms, ease-out, transform/opacity only. No bounce
    30→   except the agent’s streaming indicator.
    31→7. **Honest surfaces.** Real screenshots and real renders, not fake UI mockups. No fake
    32→   customer logos, no invented metrics, no “AI magic” copy.
    33→8. **Accessible by default.** Contrast, focus, keyboard, reduced motion and touch
    34→   targets are part of every component, not a later audit.
    35→

    37→
    38→## 2. Brand direction
    39→
    40→### 2.1 Name and voice
    41→
    42→- `Studio` is the codename. The final product name is a token swap in
    43→  `packages/config/brand.ts`; no component hardcodes it.
    44→- Voice: concise, confident, specific. Short sentences. No hype.
    45→- Say “project” and “composition”. Never “document”.
    46→- Do not use EffectCraft, ArtCraft, PhotoCraft, Photoshop or Cursor in user-facing
    47→  copy. Attribution lives in `ATTRIBUTION.md` and the licences page, not the UI.
    48→- Avoid “AI-powered” as a headline. Show what the agent does instead.
    49→
    50→### 2.2 Visual references (behaviour, not copying)
    51→
    52→- Film title cards: large serif, generous negative space, one warm accent.
    53→- Editorial magazines: rules, captions, asymmetric grids, strong hierarchy.
    54→- Pro creative tools: dark neutral surfaces, precise 1px lines, restrained states.
    55→- Not: generic AI SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, floating 3D orbs, purple-on-black,
    56→  fake terminal windows, stock photos of people at laptops.
    57→
    58→### 2.3 Signature elements
    59→
    60→1. **The frame line.** A thin horizontal rule with a small timecode label used as a
    61→   section divider (`00:00:12 — WORKFLOW`). It ties the shell to the timeline without
    62→   pretending to be the editor.
    63→2. **The signal dot.** A small accent dot for live/agent/recording state. Never a
    64→   glowing orb.
    65→3. **The slate.** A compact project card header with name, duration and comp count,
    66→   like a film slate.
    67→4. **Warm paper on carbon.** Warm off-white text on near-black surfaces; avoids the
    68→   cold blue-grey of generic dashboards.

    72→## 3. Colour
    73→
    74→All colours are CSS variables in `packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css` and mirrored in
    75→Tailwind theme tokens. Components use semantic tokens, never raw hex.
    76→
    77→### 3.1 Core palette
    78→
    79→| Token | Hex | Use |
    80→|---|---|---|
    81→| `--ink-0` | `#070809` | Page background, editor surround |
    82→| `--ink-1` | `#0C0E10` | App background |
    83→| `--ink-2` | `#121517` | Panel / card |
    84→| `--ink-3` | `#181C1F` | Elevated surface, hover |
    85→| `--ink-4` | `#20262A` | Input, pressed |
    86→| `--line-subtle` | `#232A2E` | Hairlines, dividers |
    87→| `--line-strong` | `#333C41` | Focused borders, strong dividers |
    88→| `--paper` | `#F3F0E8` | Primary text |
    89→| `--paper-dim` | `#C9C6BE` | Secondary text |
    90→| `--ash` | `#8E969C` | Muted text, metadata |
    91→| `--ash-dim` | `#5E686E` | Disabled text |
    92→| `--signal` | `#FF6A3D` | Primary action, live state, selection |
    93→| `--signal-hover` | `#FF805C` | Hover |
    94→| `--signal-press` | `#E8562B` | Pressed |
    95→| `--signal-ink` | `#1A0B06` | Text on signal |
    96→| `--ice` | `#8FD3E8` | Informational, links, focus alternative |
    97→| `--success` | `#7BD88F` | Applied, saved, healthy |
    98→| `--warning` | `#F2C14E` | Warning, credit low |
    99→| `--danger` | `#FF6B6B` | Destructive, failed |
   100→| `--violet` | `#B8A1FF` | Reserved for version/comment accents only |
   101→
   102→**Rules**
   103→
   104→- The accent is for **one primary action per view**. Do not use it for every link.
   105→- Backgrounds stay within `ink-0..ink-3`; do not introduce blue-grey surfaces.
   106→- No pure `#000` or `#FFF`. The warm paper is deliberate.
   107→- No gradient text. No accent gradients. A single low-opacity radial atmosphere is
   108→  allowed on the landing hero only.
   109→- Status is never colour-only: pair with an icon and a label.
   110→
   111→### 3.2 Semantic tokens
   112→
   113→| Token | Value | Use |
   114→|---|---|---|
   115→| `--bg-page` | `--ink-0` | Page |
   116→| `--bg-app` | `--ink-1` | App shell |
   117→| `--bg-panel` | `--ink-2` | Cards, chat panel |
   118→| `--bg-elevated` | `--ink-3` | Popovers, menus, hover |
   119→| `--bg-input` | `--ink-4` | Inputs |
   120→| `--text-primary` | `--paper` | Headings, body |
   121→| `--text-secondary` | `--paper-dim` | Supporting copy |
   122→| `--text-muted` | `--ash` | Metadata |
   123→| `--text-disabled` | `--ash-dim` | Disabled |
   124→| `--border` | `--line-subtle` | Default border |
   125→| `--border-strong` | `--line-strong` | Hover/focus border |
   126→| `--focus-ring` | `--signal` | Focus ring (2px offset 2px) |
   127→| `--overlay` | `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.65)` | Modal scrim |
   128→
   129→### 3.3 Contrast targets
   130→
   131→- Body text on `ink-1`: ≥ 7:1.
   132→- Metadata on `ink-2`: ≥ 4.5:1.
   133→- Accent text on `ink-1`: ≥ 4.5:1; if not, use accent as a fill with `signal-ink`.
   134→- Disabled text may be below 4.5:1 but must still be legible and never the only
   135→  indicator of state.
   136→- Test with APCA where possible; WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor.

   140→## 4. Typography
   141→
   142→### 4.1 Families
   143→
   144→| Role | Family | Fallback | Notes |
   145→|---|---|---|---|
   146→| Display | **Instrument Serif** | Georgia, serif | Hero, section titles, large numbers |
   147→| UI / body | **Instrument Sans** | system-ui, sans-serif | Buttons, nav, forms, chat |
   148→| Mono | **JetBrains Mono** | ui-monospace | Timecode, code, keyboard keys |
   149→
   150→Load with `next/font/google`, `display: swap`, subset latin. Self-host in production if
   151→the licence/ops requires it. Do not commit font binaries without a licence file and
   152→attribution.
   153→
   154→### 4.2 Scale
   155→
   156→| Token | Size / line-height | Use |
   157→|---|---|---|
   158→| `display-xl` | 72 / 0.95, tracking -0.03em | Landing hero only, desktop |
   159→| `display-l` | 56 / 1.0, tracking -0.02em | Landing sections |
   160→| `display-m` | 40 / 1.05, tracking -0.02em | Page titles |
   161→| `title-l` | 28 / 1.15, tracking -0.01em | Panel titles |
   162→| `title-m` | 20 / 1.25 | Card titles |
   163→| `body-l` | 17 / 1.6 | Marketing body |
   164→| `body` | 15 / 1.6 | App body |
   165→| `body-s` | 13 / 1.5 | Dense UI, chat |
   166→| `label` | 12 / 1.2, tracking 0.06em, uppercase | Overlines, table headers |
   167→| `mono` | 12 / 1.5 | Timecode, code |
   168→
   169→Rules:
   170→
   171→- Display serif is for **short** headings, never body copy or long paragraphs.
   172→- Do not use all-caps for sentences; only labels and timecode.
   173→- Numbers in stats use tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
   174→- Max line length: 68ch for prose, 80ch for docs.
   175→- No text smaller than 12px in the app; 11px only for keyboard hints.
   176→
   177→### 4.3 Type colour and emphasis
   178→
   179→- Headings: `text-primary`.
   180→- Section overlines: `text-muted` + `label`.
   181→- Accent words inside a headline: use `text-signal` sparingly, not a gradient.
   182→- Links: `text-primary` with an underline offset 3px; hover `text-signal`.
   183→- Never use font weight 300 on dark backgrounds; it thins out. Use 400/500.
   184→

   187→## 5. Space, grid and shape
   188→
   189→### 5.1 Spacing scale
   190→
   191→`4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128`
   192→
   193→- Base unit 4px.
   194→- Component padding: 12–16px compact, 20–24px comfortable.
   195→- Page gutters: 20px mobile, 32px tablet, 48px desktop, 64px wide.
   196→- Marketing max width: 1200px; app max width: 1440px; prose max width: 720px.
   197→
   198→### 5.2 Grid
   199→
   200→- 12 columns desktop, 24px gutter.
   201→- 8 columns tablet, 20px gutter.
   202→- 4 columns mobile, 16px gutter.
   203→- Asymmetry is allowed on marketing (7/5 or 5/7 splits), not in forms or tables.
   204→- Do not center everything. Editorial layouts use a strong left edge.
   205→
   206→### 5.3 Radii
   207→
   208→| Token | Value | Use |
   209→|---|---|---|
   210→| `radius-xs` | 3px | Badges, keys |
   211→| `radius-sm` | 6px | Inputs, buttons, tool cards |
   212→| `radius-md` | 10px | Cards, popovers |
   213→| `radius-lg` | 14px | Modals, large panels |
   214→| `radius-pill` | 999px | Pills, avatars |
   215→
   216→The editor has its own density; the chat panel uses `radius-md`. Do not use 24px+
   217→rounded cards everywhere.
   218→
   219→### 5.4 Borders and elevation
   220→
   221→- Default: `1px solid var(--border)`.
   222→- Hover: border `--border-strong`; no size change.
   223→- Elevation 1 (popover): `0 8px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / .35)`.
   224→- Elevation 2 (modal): `0 24px 64px rgb(0 0 0 / .55)`.
   225→- Chat panel: elevation 2 + 1px border; no glass blur.
   226→- Never use a coloured glow as elevation.
   227→

   229→
   230→## 6. Motion
   231→
   232→### 6.1 Timing
   233→
   234→| Token | Value | Use |
   235→|---|---|---|
   236→| `motion-fast` | 120ms | Hover, press, focus |
   237→| `motion-base` | 180ms | Menus, popovers, tab changes |
   238→| `motion-slow` | 240ms | Sheets, page transitions |
   239→| `ease-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` | Entrances |
   240→| `ease-in-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` | Moves |
   241→
   242→### 6.2 Rules
   243→
   244→- Animate only `transform`, `opacity`, `filter` (sparingly). Avoid animating layout.
   245→- No page-load choreography that delays content. Hero type may fade/translate 8px once.
   246→- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: remove transforms, keep opacity changes ≤ 100ms.
   247→- Streaming text uses a blinking caret, not a typewriter animation.
   248→- Toasts: slide 8px + fade, 180ms; auto-dismiss 5s, pause on hover.
   249→- Never auto-play a heavy video on mobile; use a poster + play button.
   250→
   251→### 6.3 Signature motion
   252→
   253→- **Frame line draw:** 240ms scaleX from 0 to 1 on scroll into view, once.
   254→- **Slate shuffle:** project cards reveal thumbnail with a 4px upward fade, 40ms stagger,
   255→  max 8 cards.
   256→- **Agent pulse:** a 1.2s opacity pulse on the signal dot while streaming; stops when
   257→  done.

   261→## 7. Components
   262→
   263→All components live in `packages/ui`. They are headless Radix primitives + Tailwind +
   264→CVA variants. Every component has a Storybook-style example and an axe test.
   265→
   266→### 7.1 Button
   267→
   268→| Variant | Use | Style |
   269→|---|---|---|
   270→| `primary` | One main action | Signal fill, `signal-ink` text |
   271→| `secondary` | Supporting action | `ink-3` fill, 1px border |
   272→| `ghost` | Toolbar/quiet | Transparent, hover `ink-3` |
   273→| `danger` | Destructive | Danger fill or danger text + border |
   274→| `link` | Inline navigation | Text + underline |
   275→
   276→Sizes: `sm` 28px, `md` 36px, `lg` 44px. Icon-only buttons are 32/36px with a tooltip
   277→and `aria-label`. Loading keeps width; spinner replaces icon, label becomes “Working…”.
   278→Disabled uses `opacity: .45` and `cursor: not-allowed`, plus `aria-disabled`.
   279→
   280→### 7.2 Input, textarea, select
   281→
   282→- Height 36px (sm 32, lg 44); 8px horizontal padding; `radius-sm`.
   283→- Background `--bg-input`; border `--border`; focus ring 2px signal at 2px offset.
   284→- Labels above, 12px `label` style, not placeholder-only.
   285→- Helper text 12px muted; error text danger + icon.
   286→- Textarea min 3 rows, auto-grow in chat.
   287→- Select uses a custom popover, not native on desktop; native on mobile is acceptable.
   288→- Search input has a leading icon and `⌘K` hint.
   289→
   290→### 7.3 Card
   291→
   292→- Background `--bg-panel`, 1px border, `radius-md`, padding 20–24px.
   293→- No drop shadow at rest; hover raises border and background one step.
   294→- Project cards are the exception: thumbnail dominates, metadata below, hover reveals
   295→  actions. Aspect ratio 16/9 for thumbnails; 4/3 for templates.
   296→- Card title 16–18px medium; metadata 12px muted.
   297→
   298→### 7.4 Tabs
   299→
   300→- Underline tabs for page sections; pill tabs for filters.
   301→- Active tab uses paper text + 2px signal underline; inactive muted.
   302→- Keyboard: arrow keys move, Home/End jump, focus visible.
   303→
   304→### 7.5 Dialog / sheet
   305→
   306→- Radix Dialog. Scrim `--overlay`; panel `--bg-panel`, `radius-lg`, max width 560px
   307→  (dialog) or 720px (large).
   308→- Title 20px; description 14px muted; actions right-aligned, primary last.
   309→- Mobile: bottom sheet with drag handle, max-height 90vh, focus trapped.
   310→- Destructive dialogs require the action name, not just “OK”.
   311→
   312→### 7.6 Popover / menu / tooltip
   313→
   314→- Popover: `--bg-elevated`, 1px border, `radius-md`, 8px padding, elevation 1.
   315→- Menu item height 32px; icon 16px; shortcut right-aligned in mono 11px.
   316→- Tooltip: 250ms delay, 12px, `ink-4` background, paper text, no arrow (or a subtle
   317→  one), never the only source of an accessible name.
   318→
   319→### 7.7 Toast
   320→
   321→- Bottom-right on desktop, above the chat if open; top-center on mobile.
   322→- Variants: default, success, warning, danger.
   323→- Title + optional description; one action max; close button.
   324→- Never use a toast for an error that requires a decision; use an inline banner/dialog.
   325→
   326→### 7.8 Badge / status
   327→
   328→- 20px height, 6px horizontal padding, `radius-xs`, 11px uppercase label.
   329→- Variants: neutral, signal, success, warning, danger, ice.
   330→- Status dot + label for live states.
   331→- Risk badges in chat: low = neutral/success, medium = warning, high = danger.
   332→
   333→### 7.9 Table
   334→
   335→- Header 12px uppercase muted; rows 44–52px; 1px dividers.
   336→- Numeric columns right-aligned, tabular numerals.
   337→- Row hover `--ink-3`; selected row has a 2px signal left border.
   338→- Sticky header on long tables; horizontal scroll with a visible edge fade.
   339→- Empty state inside the table body, not a separate blank page.
   340→
   341→### 7.10 Command palette
   342→
   343→- `Cmd/Ctrl+K` in the SaaS shell (not while the editor iframe has focus; the editor
   344→  keeps its own shortcuts).
   345→- 560px wide, top 20vh, search + grouped results, keyboard-first.
   346→- Groups: Projects, Actions, Navigation, Help.
   347→- Shows shortcut hints; `Enter` runs, `Esc` closes.
   348→
   349→### 7.11 Credit meter
   350→
   351→- A compact horizontal meter with used/included and a top-up CTA.
   352→- States: healthy (ice/success), low (warning), exhausted (danger).
   353→- Always show the numeric balance next to the meter; never colour-only.
   354→- In the chat footer, show estimated run cost, not the whole balance.
   355→
   356→### 7.12 Empty states
   357→
   358→- One clear sentence, one primary action, one secondary link.
   359→- Optional line illustration in 1px `--line-strong`; no 3D characters.
   360→- Example: “No projects yet. Start from a composition or import an `.ecproj`.”
   361→
   362→### 7.13 Skeletons
   363→
   364→- Use only for content that is actually loading; no skeleton for the whole app.
   365→- Background `--ink-3`, 1px radius, 1.4s shimmer at 6% opacity. Respect reduced motion.
   366→- Preserve final layout dimensions to avoid shift.

   370→## 8. Page-by-page design
   371→
   372→### 8.1 Landing (`/`)
   373→
   374→**Goal:** in five seconds, show that this is a serious motion tool with an agent that
   375→edits real compositions.
   376→
   377→```text
   378→┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   379→│  ◐ Studio        Product  Agents  Work  Pricing        Sign in  → │
   380→├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   381→│                                                                    │
   382→│   MOTION, DIRECTED BY YOU                         [ live editor ]  │
   383→│                                                                    │
   384→│   A composition editor with an agent that understands              │
   385→│   layers, keyframes and taste.                                     │
   386→│                                                                    │
   387→│   [ Start 14-day trial ]   [ Watch the 90s film ]                  │
   388→│   Card required · Cancel anytime · No free tier                    │
   389→│                                                                    │
   390→│   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐   │
   391→│   │  real editor capture / looping title-sequence demo         │   │
   392→│   │  with chat overlay visible                                │   │
   393→│   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘   │
   394→│                                                                    │
   395→│   00:00:01 — THE WORK                                              │
   396→│   Frame-accurate timeline. Real layers. Editable output.           │
   397→└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   398→```
   399→
   400→Sections:
   401→
   402→1. **Hero** — serif headline, one sentence, two CTAs, real demo. No gradient text.
   403→2. **Proof strip** — short factual capabilities, not fake logos:
   404→   “Keyframes · 3D · 306 effects · agent edits · version history”.
   405→3. **The agent** — a three-step story: ask → review plan → apply/undo. Show real chat
   406→   cards. Make the approval model visible.
   407→4. **The editor** — full-bleed screenshot with callouts. Say “a full compositing
   408→   editor, unmodified” — **never** use the upstream brand name in the marketing UI.
   409→   Attribution lives in the licences page, not the hero.
   410→5. **Workflow** — import → compose → animate → review → render.
   411→6. **Collaboration** — comments, versions, presence. No fake avatars; use initials.
   412→7. **Performance** — GPU-accelerated, browser-native, no install. Real numbers only.
   413→8. **Pricing** — three plans + top-ups; no “free forever”.
   414→9. **FAQ** — honest answers about browser support, file formats, data, models.
   415→10. **Final CTA** — “Start the 14-day trial.” Card required.
   416→11. **Footer** — product, resources, legal, licences, status, changelog.
   417→
   418→Rules:
   419→
   420→- Hero art is real product output, not a stylised fake UI.
   421→- No autoplay audio. Video is muted, looped, ≤ 8s, with a pause control.
   422→- Every claim has evidence or is removed.
   423→- Mobile hero: 40px serif, full-width CTA, demo becomes a poster + play.
   424→- LCP element is the headline or poster, never a heavy video.
   425→
   426→### 8.2 Auth (`/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, `/invite/:token`)
   427→
   428→Split layout: left 55% form on `--bg-app`; right 45% a single cinematic still or a
   429→loop from the demo, with a thin frame line and a short quote. On mobile, the image is a
   430→16/9 banner above the form.
   431→
   432→- Heading: “Sign in to Studio.” / “Create your workspace.”
   433→- OAuth buttons: Google, GitHub, full width, 44px, with brand marks in monochrome.
   434→- Divider: “or continue with email”.
   435→- Email + password; show/hide; forgot password.
   436→- Sign-up: name, email, password, terms checkbox. No marketing opt-in pre-checked.
   437→- Invite: show workspace name, inviter, role; accept/decline. If signed out, redirect
   438→  back after auth.
   439→- Error states inline, not toasts.
   440→- Trial copy: “14 days free, then $29/mo. Card required today. Cancel anytime.”
   441→- No social proof carousel on auth. One still is enough.
   442→
   443→### 8.3 Workspace creation / onboarding
   444→
   445→Three steps, one card, no wizard chrome:
   446→
   447→1. **Workspace** — name + URL slug; availability check.
   448→2. **Use case** — motion graphics, title design, social video, product film, other.
   449→3. **Start** — “Create workspace” or “Start trial” (Stripe Checkout). Card required.
   450→
   451→- Progress is a 3-segment line, not numbered circles.
   452→- Skip is allowed for use case, not for workspace name.
   453→- After creation, land on the dashboard with a “Create your first project” card.
   454→- Do not ask for a credit card before the workspace exists; do it at trial start.
   455→

   515→### 8.7 Editor (`/w/projects/:id/edit`)
   516→
   517→**This page must not restyle EffectCraft.** Full-bleed editor, minimal outer chrome.
   518→
   519→```text
   520→┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   521→│ ◐  Acme / Launch film   ·  Saved 12s ago      [Share]  [◉ 2]  [⋯] │  floating bar, auto-hide
   522→├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

   525→│                                                                    │

   528→│                                                │                │  │
   529→│                                                └────────────────┘  │
   530→└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   532→
   533→- The editor is the page. No sidebar by default; no persistent SaaS nav.
   534→- A **floating project bar** at the top-left (or top-center) shows workspace/project
   535→  breadcrumb, save state, presence, Share, and a `⋯` menu. It auto-hides after 2.5s of
   536→  pointer inactivity and returns on move/focus. It must not cover the editor's menu bar
   537→  when visible; place it below the editor's top bar or make it a compact pill.
   538→- A **floating back button** returns to the project hub after confirming unsaved
   539→  changes.
   540→- Chat floats bottom-right by default; sidebar mode shrinks the iframe.
   541→- The editor's own menus, panels, timeline and shortcuts are untouched.
   542→- Browser-level unsaved changes guard; autosave runs regardless.
   543→- Read-only mode: a small “View only” badge; editor remains usable for inspection but
   544→  mutations are blocked by the lease and gateway.
   545→- Unsupported browser/GPU: full-page message with supported browsers and a static
   546→  preview; do not trap the user in a broken canvas.
   547→- Loading: dark frame with the project name, a thin progress line and “Preparing
   548→  editor…”, then the editor. No fake skeleton of the editor UI.

   554→- Do not hide or rearrange EffectCraft panels.
   555→- Do not add a second timeline, toolbar or preview.

   654→## 12. Accessibility
   655→
   656→- WCAG 2.2 AA minimum; target AAA for body text where practical.
   657→- Full keyboard access: skip link, visible focus, logical order, no traps.
   658→- Focus ring: 2px signal, 2px offset; never `outline: none` without a replacement.
   659→- Hit targets: 44×44px on touch, 32×32px minimum on desktop.
   660→- Forms: labels, descriptions, errors linked via `aria-describedby`; errors announced.
   661→- Dialogs/sheets: focus trap, `Esc` closes, restore focus.
   662→- Chat: `aria-live="polite"` for assistant messages; `role="log"`; stop button
   663→  reachable; tool cards are buttons with `aria-expanded`.
   664→- Motion: `prefers-reduced-motion`; no flashing > 3 Hz; no parallax.
   665→- Colour: never the only indicator; test protanopia/deuteranopia.
   666→- Zoom: usable at 200%; no horizontal scroll at 320px for the shell.
   667→- Captions for demo video; transcripts for the launch film.
   668→- Alt text for meaningful images; empty alt for decorative.

   702→## 15. Implementation notes
   703→
   704→- Tailwind config maps semantic tokens; no arbitrary hex in components.
   705→- `class-variance-authority` for variants; `tailwind-merge` for overrides.
   706→- Radix primitives for dialog, popover, menu, tabs, tooltip, select, toast.
   707→- Use CSS variables for theme; dark is default and only theme in v1.
   708→- `next/font` for fonts; no layout shift.
   709→- Component files: `packages/ui/src/components/<name>.tsx`; stories and tests beside.
   710→- Use `data-state` attributes for open/closed/active states.
   711→- No component may import app code; the package is standalone.
   712→- The chat overlay lives in `apps/web/src/components/editor/chat` and composes
   713→  `packages/ui`, not the other way round.
