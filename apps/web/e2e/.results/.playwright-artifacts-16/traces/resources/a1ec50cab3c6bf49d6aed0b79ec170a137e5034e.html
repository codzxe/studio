00:00:02 — TOP-UPS
100 credits·500 credits·2,000 credits

One-time packs, never a subscription. Pack prices live in Stripe and are shown at checkout — we never auto-charge a top-up.

00:00:03 — THE TRIAL

14 days free, then $12/mo. Card required today. Cancel anytime.

The trial is a state of the Starter plan, not a separate tier: 50 credits, 1 seat, 3 projects, 5 render minutes a month and the Fast model.

There is no free tier. Cancel during the trial and the card is not charged at trial end.

00:00:04 — RENDER FORMATS

Renders export as MP4 (H.264) or PNG sequence today. ProRes, GIF, WAV audio are on the format list but not enabled yet — they appear the day the render worker supports them.

END — PRICING

Plans are billed monthly through Stripe; you can change or cancel from billing any time and your work is never deleted for cancelling. Prices shown are the working beta numbers — finalised at launch, and always on this page. Questions about what a plan covers? Read the FAQ or write to us.
